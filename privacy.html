Mac Mini Backup · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Privacy policy

This policy describes Mac Mini Backup, a personal-use backup utility maintained by jmulgogi.

Data accessed and used

The utility processes files selected by its owner and the Google Drive file content and metadata needed to upload, list, retrieve, and manage those backups. It uses Google OAuth credentials to perform authorized operations. With the intended drive.file scope, access is limited to files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it.

Storage and sharing

Backup archives are stored locally and in the owner's Google Drive. rclone stores authorization credentials in its local configuration on the owner's computer. This informational website does not receive those credentials or backups. The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties other than Google as required to provide storage.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls backup retention and can delete local archives and Google Drive copies. Files in Google Drive trash may remain until the trash is emptied or Google removes them under its retention rules. The owner can revoke authorization in their Google Account connections settings and remove the local rclone configuration. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete existing backups.

Website hosting

This website has no analytics scripts, forms, or advertising cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data under its privacy statement.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in the website repository. Do not include credentials, backup contents, or other private information in public issues.
